Tips for good results
  • Dark pen on white, unlined paper, photographed in even light without shadows.
  • Crop the photo to just the handwriting. Leave clear space between lines.
  • The model learned from ~650 people's handwriting (the IAM dataset). Your own writing may give more mistakes until you fine-tune the model on it (see README, section 9).
